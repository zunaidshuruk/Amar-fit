## Bug — the view model's "today" is frozen when the app starts (Part AS, 2026-10-12)
Zunaid (screenshot of the Sleep tab showing 6.5 h and a Last 7 Nights chart with one column missing): sleep entered on the Sleep screen shows "No data" on the Today screen. **Cause (code-checked at `9d41fd1`):** `ShasthoViewModel.kt` line 240: `val todayDateString = SimpleDateFormat("yyyy-MM-dd", Locale.US).format(Date())` is evaluated ONCE when the view model is created. If the app process stays alive past midnight (very common) "today" stays the day the process started. Everything built on it is stale: `todayMetrics` (observes the old row), `todayFoodLogs`, `todayActivityEvents` and `metricsHistory` (90-day window start) are flows created once, and all 28 uses of `todayDateString` write to the OLD date (`setSleep`, `addWater`, `addSteps`, glucose, blood pressure, weight, manual exercise, workout and mindfulness saves, the Health Connect sync merge). The Sleep, Health, Nutrition, Food Log, Glucose, Weight and Edit Focus screens read those stale flows, so they show the entry; the Today screen computes the real date (`LocalDate.now()`), reads a row that was never written and shows "No data"; the Last 7 Nights chart (real dates) has no column for the real today. This probably also explains part of the earlier "water does nothing" report. **Fix:** `todayDateString` becomes a live getter; the today-bound flows follow a `todayDate` state through `flatMapLatest`; a midnight ticker and an ON_RESUME call refresh it; the Today screen's selected date resets to the new today. Entries already written under old dates stay there (the user re-enters sleep once).

## Sprint 17 — Multi-device sync (Part AT, built, released in 2.7 (build 27), **two-phone test PASSED by Zunaid 2026-10-13**)
Zunaid (two phones on one account, screenshots 2026-10-12 23:46): one phone shows 21 steps, the other 9,209; Drive backup from the 9,209 phone and restore on the other changed nothing. Wanted: everything syncs between devices automatically through Firestore, restored on whichever device he signs in to; for steps and other counters the HIGHER value is right.

**Root causes (code-checked at `25067dc`):** (1) `FirebaseManager.syncMetric` is `set(metric, SetOptions.merge())` of the WHOLE DailyMetric, fire-and-forget (no await, no retry): a device writes every field including zeros, so the last writer wins and a phone with 21 steps overwrites 9,209 in the cloud, and a phone with no glucose entry writes 0 over another phone's glucose reading. (2) `pullDataOnLogin` runs only at login (called from `AppRepository` line ~144) and REPLACES local rows with `insertMetrics`/`insertProfile` (no merge); nothing pulls again while the app runs or resumes, and there are no Firestore snapshot listeners except direct messages. (3) `syncWithHealthConnect` replaces counters with the local Health Connect value (`steps = if (hcSteps > 0) hcSteps else current.steps`), so each phone re-asserts its own number every 10 seconds, which also undid the Drive restore. (4) The profile has the same last-writer-wins problem (`syncProfile` writes all fields; a stale phone overwrites newer settings such as `navTabs`). (5) Deletions are push-only: `deleteFoodLog`, `deleteSavedDietChart` ... remove the cloud document but other devices never remove their copy and nothing pulls removals. (6) Not synced at all: assistant chat sessions and messages, exercise sessions, activity events. Synced collections today: profile, health_metrics, diet_logs, saved_diet_charts, saved_workouts, workout_programs, saved_chats, medical_records.

**Design (proposed):** one pure merge function used everywhere. `MetricMerger.merge(a, b)`: COUNTERS take the higher value (steps, distanceMeters, activeCaloriesBurned, exerciseMinutes, mindfulnessMinutes, waterLiters, caloriesConsumed, carbsG, proteinG, fatG, externalNutritionCalories, sodium, sugar, fiber); `heartRateMin` takes the smaller non-zero and `heartRateMax` the larger; READINGS (weight, blood pressure, the 8 glucose fields, sleepHours, resting heart rate, heart rate, HRV, SpO2, respiratory rate, skin temperature) prefer the non-zero side and, when both are non-zero, the row with the newer `updatedAt`; `updatedAt` = max. New `DailyMetric.updatedAt` and `UserProfile.updatedAt` (Room migration), stamped in `saveMetrics` / `saveProfile`. Cloud writes become Firestore TRANSACTIONS that read the remote row, merge and write the merged row (zeros can never overwrite data), with a retry queue of dirty dates when offline. Pulls MERGE instead of replace: full history at login (this is the restore on a new device), the last 7 days on every app resume (throttled), the last 60 days once a day, plus a realtime snapshot listener on the last 3 days so the Today screen converges across devices within seconds. Health Connect sync uses max for counters. Profile: the newer `updatedAt` wins as a whole. Deletions: soft delete flags in the cloud documents (`deleted = true`) that other devices apply and a purge after 30 days. Drive restore goes through the merger. Settings gets a "Cloud sync" row (last synced time, Sync now, errors). Firestore reads must stay inside the free tier (50k per day): quick pulls are small and throttled.

**Root causes (code-checked at `9d41fd1`):** (1) Length is capped by design: `aiResponseStyle(150, followUps = true)` in `AppRepository.kt` says one short opening sentence, at most 3 sections, at most 4 one-sentence bullets each, "keep the whole reply under about 150 words" (Part Y), and the Assistant prompt adds "Keep replies conversational and concise"; the on-device prompt says at most 150 words and caps output at 400 tokens. (2) The model gets almost no data: the Assistant prompt does not include the user's data; the model must decide to call `read_health_data`, "only when the user's real numbers would genuinely change your answer"; that tool returns ONLY 7 days and 6 fields per day (steps, sleepHours, restingHR, weightKg, waterLiters, caloriesConsumed) plus 14 food logs with just a name and calories; no glucose, no blood pressure (although the tool description claims them), no macros, no HRV or oxygen, no exercise sessions, no goals, no medical records, no trends, no 30-day view; `ShasthoViewModel` passes `getMetricsHistoryFlow(7)`. (3) Nothing is computed: trends, goal adherence, per-meal glucose rises, correlations and week-over-week deltas do not exist, so the model can only say generic things. (4) Repetition comes from overlapping tools: `get_health_insight`, `get_glucose_guidance`, `generate_diet_chart` and the model's own summary each produce a similar paragraph and nothing forbids repeating them; the style guide forces a TL;DR, a Next step and follow-ups on every reply.

**Design (proposed):** compute first, then phrase. (a) `HealthDossierBuilder` + `FindingsEngine` (pure Kotlin, unit tested): from DailyMetric (30 days), FoodLog, HealthExerciseSession, ActivityEvent and profile goals build windows 7/14/30 with averages, min/max, trend slope, goal adherence, weekday vs weekend, sleep consistency; glucose per meal (before and after breakfast, lunch, dinner), post-meal rise per meal, fasting average, time in the user's target range, highs and lows, estimated HbA1c; blood pressure average, highest, readings above the usual 130/80 reference, trend; resting heart rate, HRV and SpO2 trends; calories vs limit with macro percentages and top foods; exercise minutes, sessions and types; weight change; simple correlations only when at least 5 paired days exist (for example glucose after short sleep versus good sleep, steps versus glucose, high-carb days versus next-morning fasting glucose) reported as patterns, never as causes; and a ranked list of FINDINGS, each with severity and its evidence. (b) Gemini analyst mode: the dossier is attached automatically for health questions and returned in full by `read_health_data` (fixing the tool so it really includes glucose, blood pressure, macros, exercise and goals); a deep-answer style for review, trend, why, how-am-I-doing and advice questions (about 400 to 800 words: Snapshot, What your data shows with numbers and dates, Likely drivers, a 7-day action plan with deshi foods and workouts tied to each finding, support from the KardIQ protocol and medicinal recipes with the doctor line, What to track and when to see a doctor) and hard anti-repetition rules (every bullet adds a new number or a new action; never restate a section; never talk about the AI or Gemini; do not call get_health_insight or get_glucose_guidance on a review); casual chat stays short. (c) A "Full 30-day health review" button in the Assistant and on the Health screen. (d) On the phone: the compact dossier plus FINDINGS feed the model, and an offline review shows the code-built findings with protocol suggestions matched to each finding (no model needed, no hallucinated numbers). (e) Optional: medical records (conditions, medicines, allergies) included in the analysis behind a consent switch.

## Sprint 15 — On-device AI, "Offline AI" (Part AP, idea 2026-10-08, not scoped)
Zunaid's question: can the text AI chats run on a model built into the app, with no Gemini API? **Research (2026-10-08, Google AI Edge docs):** the supported way is **LiteRT-LM** (`com.google.ai.edge.litertlm:litertlm-android`, Kotlin API, `.litertlm` model files; it replaces the maintenance-only MediaPipe LLM Inference API); it advertises production readiness and **tool use / function calling with constrained decoding**; supported models include Gemma 4 E2B (2.58 GB), Gemma 3n E2B (2.97 GB) and E4B (4.2 GB), also Llama, Phi-4, Qwen; Google's published speed is on a flagship (Samsung S26 Ultra, Gemma 4 E2B: about 47 tokens/s on CPU, 52 on GPU), so mid-range phones will be slower; Gemini Nano through Android AICore only exists on a short list of supported phones.

**Fit for KardIQ:** good on-device: plain health chat, explaining metrics, short tips, summaries, privacy (health data never leaves the phone), offline, no rate limits or keys. Weak on-device: the Assistant's tools (accurate multi-meal logging with macros, diet charts with Bangladeshi foods, JSON programs/workouts), long outputs, Bangla quality, food-photo scanning accuracy, battery and heat. Costs: one-time 2.6 to 3 GB model download, 6 GB+ RAM recommended, storage. Delivery is a real problem to solve (the model cannot live in the APK; GitHub release assets are limited to 2 GB per file; Gemma files on Hugging Face may require accepting Google's license/token; alternatives: user imports the file, or hosting elsewhere).

**Proposed shape:** an "AI engine" setting with Cloud (Gemini, default), On-device (offline), Auto (on-device when offline or for simple chat, cloud for tools and photos), behind an `AssistantEngine` interface; opt-in "Offline AI (beta)".
